Things I learned the hard way, squeezed into cookies.

  • The first root cause is a suspect, not a verdict.
  • If it isn't monitored, it's a rumour.
  • Don't fix the bug. Make the bug impossible.
  • A language is a brush. Please stop arguing about brushes.
  • Retries are a promise. Only make them to errors that deserve one.

$ ls postmortems/

Blameless format, including when the person to blame was me. Tap one to open it.

INC-001 The Friday deploy
What happened
I shipped on a Friday. A change caused duplicate webhooks, and the dead-job queue filled up by the thousands.
Fix
Hotfix in about 90 minutes, then a blameless write-up and an anomaly monitor.
Prevention
I added a CI/CD check that blocks Friday deploys for everyone, starting with me.
INC-002 Four workers, allegedly
What happened
The config said 4 Puma workers. The servers had quietly been running 3, because a "smart" restart never re-read boot-time config.
Lesson
Check the running process, not the file.
INC-003 Falling for my first theory
What happened
On more than one investigation I was sure I'd found the cause, then had to throw the theory out when the data disagreed. Twice in one case.
Lesson
Write the hypothesis down, then try to prove it wrong before you tell anyone it's right.
INC-004 Too quiet in the room
What happened
Early on I'd have the right idea and not push for it. A colleague's feedback, in three words: "be more brave."
Fix
I started writing RFCs and leading the discussions, pushing back on vague answers, and holding a position under pressure while conceding the points that were fair.
INC-005 PR description: "see ticket"
What happened
My early pull requests were a ticket link and a prayer.
Fix
Now they carry context, a rollout plan, and a rollback plan that actually runs. Reviewers are noticeably less sad.
INC-006 Rust for everything
What happened
I started a computer-vision side project in Rust because it sounded fun, then found that the ecosystem I needed lived in Python.
Lesson
Choose the tool for where the problem lives, not for the vibe. (I still love Rust.)
INC-007 The 2× that wasn't
What happened
I estimated a search-latency fix would make it about 2× faster. Measured result: 15–25%.
Fix
Said so out loud, kept digging, and found the real bottleneck. The answer was to remove expensive work, not buy bigger machines.
Lesson
Estimates are hypotheses too. Measure, then report what you got.
